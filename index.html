Radiology Report Summariser

Paste the findings from a radiology report and the app writes the impression, the short summary a radiologist would add.

  • Cleaned about 3,900 anonymised XML reports into 3,400 training records.
  • Fine-tuned a T5 model and scored it with ROUGE (ROUGE-1 ≈ 0.39).
  • No patient data was ever committed to GitHub.

Visual Product Recommendation App

Upload a photo of a clothing item and the app shows the five most similar products from a fashion retailer's catalogue.

  • A pre-trained ResNet50 turns each image into an embedding.
  • Matches are ranked by cosine similarity, with typical scores above 0.7.
  • Invalid and duplicate images were removed before building the catalogue.
